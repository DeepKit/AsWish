## (b) Self-approval / self-signing hole in H4 waiver / approval policy

The material exposes two separate concerns:

1. **Waiver ledger is empty** (`"total": 0`), so no live waiver is self-signed. The stated max is `max_ongoing_waivers: 0`, and the gate claims to fail closed on any introduced violation.
2. **But the authorized approver list itself contains an impersonation-pattern collision.** `authorized_approvers` is `["MasterController", "ArchitectureReviewBoard"]`, while `impersonation_patterns` is `["^(MasterController|SPW|MainController)-.*$"]`. `MasterController` is a *bare* identity here, not matching the pattern (patterns require a `-suffix`), so the pattern wouldn't catch it — but the approver identity is exactly one of the impersonated tokens the gate is designed to guard against. The schema lists both `submitted_by` and `approved_by`, and the material records `with_submitted_by: 0` — meaning **the governance control that would separate proposer from approver has never been exercised** on this run. A future waiver proposing `submitted_by: MasterController, approved_by: MasterController` would be admitted by the policy as written, because the material contains no rule forbidding `submitted_by == approved_by`.

5. **Manifest vs run reconciliation:** all 12 `manifest_declared_gates` appear in `recorded_gate_verdicts` with matching `gate_id` and `required: true`. No gate silently dropped or downgraded.
